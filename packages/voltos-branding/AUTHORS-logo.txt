The VoltOS logo is currently Tux, the Linux mascot.

Tux.svg from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tux.svg),
originally drawn by Larry Ewing <lewing@isc.tamu.edu> with The GIMP.
Permission to use and/or modify the image is granted provided Larry Ewing
and The GIMP are acknowledged.

VoltOS's copy only widens the drawing's canvas (viewBox) to a square so it
fits icon slots; the artwork itself is unchanged.
